# Trademark Policy

HyCanvas's code is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). Its name and logos are not. This policy explains how you may use the HyCanvas and HyScaler marks, so that anyone can tell an official HyCanvas from a modified or unofficial one.

## The marks

"HyCanvas" and the HyCanvas logo are trademarks of HyScaler. This policy also covers the HyScaler name and logo. Together they are "the marks".

The AGPL and the commercial license grant rights in the code only. Neither grants any right to use the marks, except as this policy allows.

## Allowed without asking

- **Referring to the project.** Saying truthfully that your product or service is "based on HyCanvas", "a fork of HyCanvas", "compatible with HyCanvas", or that you "host HyCanvas", in plain text and without the logos.
- **Unmodified official builds.** Running or redistributing official, unmodified HyCanvas releases under their own name.
- **Articles, tutorials, talks, and reviews** about HyCanvas, including using the name and screenshots, as long as it is clear they are not from HyScaler.
- **Community use** such as user groups or meetups, with names like "HyCanvas Users Bangalore", provided they are non-commercial and do not suggest they are official.

## Not allowed without written permission

- **Naming a modified version HyCanvas.** A fork or a modified build must use a different name and logo, and must not use a name that is confusingly similar (for example "HyCanvas Pro" or "HyKanvas").
- **Offering a hosted service as HyCanvas.** A hosted or managed service may say it is "powered by HyCanvas", but must not be called HyCanvas, use the logos as its own branding, or use the marks in its domain name.
- **Suggesting endorsement.** Implying that HyScaler sponsors, certifies, or supports your product, service, or fork.
- **Merchandise** or any other commercial goods bearing the marks.
- **Altered logos.** Modifying the logos, or combining them with other marks.

## Renaming a fork

If you redistribute a modified version, replace the HyCanvas name and logos in the user interface, the page titles, the favicon, and the published packages and images with your own, and remove the "HyCanvas is a HyScaler product" line on the sign-in page. Keep the copyright and license notices as the AGPL requires; those notices are not trademark use. You may say that your project is derived from HyCanvas.

## Permission and questions

To ask for permission, or to report misuse, write to licensing@hycanvas.com.

This policy may be updated from time to time; the version in this repository is the current one.
